If your report shows **FAQ Schema: Page has Q&A content but no FAQPage schema**, the scanner found question-style content and no `FAQPage` JSON-LD in the page HTML. This guide shows how to fix it in a few minutes. It is part of the checklist in our [technical AEO guide](/blog/technical-aeo-guide).

## What triggers the warning

The check passes in two cases: the page already has `FAQPage` markup, or the page has no FAQ-like content at all. It warns when it sees Q&A signals without the markup. Those signals are question marks, "how to", "what is" or "why" in your headings, or the word "FAQ" in the first part of the page text.

The warning is about machine readability. Your visitors see the same page either way.

## Step 1: Write real questions and answers

Pick 3 to 6 questions your customers actually ask. Put them on the page as visible text, each with a direct answer of one to three sentences. The answer should stand on its own, because that is the unit an answer engine is most likely to lift.

## Step 2: Generate the JSON-LD

Use the free [FAQ schema generator](/tools/faq-schema-generator) to paste your questions and answers and copy the output. The result looks like this:

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "How long does setup take?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Most teams finish setup in under an hour."
      }
    }
  ]
}
</script>
```

Place the script in the page `<head>` or body. It must be in the served HTML, not injected only after a client-side render, because many crawlers do not run JavaScript.

## Step 3: Keep markup and page text identical

Each `name` must match a question visible on the page, and each `text` must match the visible answer. Do not add questions to the JSON-LD that visitors cannot see.

## Step 4: Validate and rescan

1. Paste your URL into the [FAQ schema validator](/tools/faq-schema-validator) to confirm the markup parses and every question has an answer.
2. Run the [AEOCheck scanner](/#scanner) on the same URL. The FAQ Schema check should now pass.

## When not to add it

If the page is not really a Q&A page, such as a product page that only has a heading ending in a question mark, do not force FAQ markup. Rephrase the heading or accept the warning. Only mark up content you genuinely present as questions and answers.

## FAQ

**Why does my scan warn about FAQ schema when I have no FAQ section?** Question-style headings or the word "FAQ" near the top of the page count as Q&A content. Rephrase them or add a real, marked-up FAQ.

**Can I mark up questions that are not visible on the page?** No. The JSON-LD must match visible text.

**Does FAQPage schema guarantee citations in AI answers?** No. It improves clarity for machines, not a promise of citation.

**How long until the warning disappears?** Rescan after deploying. The check reads the live page.
