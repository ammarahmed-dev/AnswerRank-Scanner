AI search is changing how buyers discover brands. Instead of scanning ten links, users now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI tools for direct answers. That means your website needs to be understandable, citeable, and answer-ready - and you need a tool to tell you where it currently falls short.

An AEO page checker inspects technical and content conditions such as structured data, metadata, crawler access and answer structure. These checks can identify work to do, but a readiness score is not evidence that an AI system cited or recommended your brand.

This guide covers the best AEO tools available, what to look for when choosing one, and what you can do for free versus what requires a paid plan.

## What is an AEO tool?

An AEO tool helps you evaluate whether your website is ready for answer engines and AI search systems. It focuses on the signals AI systems use to decide what to understand, summarize, and cite.

Most AEO tools overlap with SEO tools, but the goal is different. Traditional SEO asks where you rank. AEO asks whether your content gets surfaced in AI answers.

For a full introduction to the concept, see [what is AEO and how it works](/blog/what-is-answer-engine-optimization).

### Evaluate an AEO checker in five minutes

1. Scan a public page whose title, schema and crawler settings you can inspect yourself.
2. Compare the findings with the page source and the relevant tool output. An unavailable measurement should remain unavailable.
3. Review whether the suggested fix fits the page. Article or FAQ markup should describe real visible content.
4. Fix one issue and rescan the same URL. Save the original report and methodology so later comparisons are meaningful.
5. Check actual business outcomes separately: Search Console clicks, referrals and recorded AI citations. A higher heuristic score does not prove those outcomes improved.

## What to look for in an AEO checker

Not all AEO tools are built equally. A reliable AEO checker should cover:

- **Structured data coverage** - does it check for JSON-LD schema, not just meta tags?
- **Content clarity scoring** - does it evaluate whether sections are written for AI extraction?
- **Crawler access verification** - does it confirm GPTBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot are allowed?
- **Authority and trust signals** - does it check for named authors, About pages, and E-E-A-T signals?
- **Scoring methodology** - does it give a numeric score so you can track improvement over time?
- **Multi-page audit capability** - can it scan an entire site, not just a single URL?
- **Monitoring over time** - does it alert you when scores drop or signals regress?
- **Actionable recommendations** - does it tell you what to fix, not just what is broken?

If a tool misses most of these, you may get partial insight but not a reliable readiness picture.

## AEO scanner vs AI visibility tracker

An **AEO scanner** checks whether a page is technically and semantically ready for AI search. It answers questions like:

- Is schema present and useful?
- Are metadata and headings clear?
- Is crawler access configured correctly?
- Does the page provide answer-ready context?

An **AI visibility tracker** monitors how often your brand appears in AI answers over time.

Most teams need both eventually. For many teams, scanners are the best starting point because they show what to fix first.
