## What Is AEO?

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the practice of structuring your content and website so that AI-powered search tools select it as a cited source when generating answers to user questions.

Unlike traditional SEO, which asks "where does my page rank?", AEO asks "does my brand get mentioned when someone asks a relevant question?"

AI answer engines include:
- **ChatGPT** (800+ million weekly users as of early 2026)
- **Perplexity** (growing fast as a research-first AI)
- **Google AI Overviews** (appearing in over 50% of all Google searches)
- **Microsoft Copilot** (powered by Bing's index)
- **Google Gemini**

These tools generate direct answers. They do not show ten links - they show one synthesized response with a small number of cited sources. Being one of those sources is the new SEO.

---

## AEO vs SEO

They are not opposites. AEO builds on the same foundation as SEO. But they optimize for different outcomes.

| | SEO | AEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in search results | Be cited in AI answers |
| Measures | Rankings, clicks, traffic | Mentions, citations, AI referrals |
| Content focus | Keywords and backlinks | Structure, authority, answer-readiness |
| Primary platforms | Google, Bing | ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews |

The good news: most AEO best practices also improve SEO. Well-structured, authoritative content ranks better in Google and gets cited more often by AI engines. You do not have to choose between them.

## How Answer Engines Choose What to Cite

Understanding the process helps you optimize for it. AI answer engines use a method called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):

**Stage 1 - Query interpretation**
The AI parses what the user is really asking - not keyword matching, but understanding intent, entities, and context.

**Stage 2 - Retrieval**
The system searches its index for pages semantically relevant to the query. It pulls candidate pages based on conceptual similarity, not just keyword overlap.

**Stage 3 - Ranking and selection**
Retrieved pages are scored on relevance, authority, recency, and structural quality. Only the highest-scoring pages make it into the final answer.

**Stage 4 - Answer generation**
The AI synthesizes a response using the selected sources and cites them.

Your job is to be in the candidate set at Stage 2, and then score highly enough at Stage 3 to make it into the final answer.

## AEO Checklist

Use this practical checklist for AI search optimization:

### 1. Technical accessibility
AI crawlers must be able to access your site. Many sites accidentally block GPTBot, PerplexityBot, or ClaudeBot in their `robots.txt` file - making them completely invisible to AI search, regardless of content quality. Fix this first.

### 2. Content structure
AI engines extract 40-75 word chunks from pages to use as answers. Content written in long unbroken paragraphs is harder to cite accurately. Use:
- Question-format H2/H3 headings
- Direct one-sentence answers at the start of each section
- FAQ sections on relevant pages
- Short, clear paragraphs

### 3. Schema and metadata
JSON-LD structured data tells AI engines exactly what your page is about, who wrote it, and what type of content it contains. Pages with schema markup are cited significantly more often than pages without it. Priority types: Organization, Article, FAQPage, WebPage.

### 4. Trust and authority signals
Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals tell AI engines they can trust your content. This includes named authorship, About and Contact pages, publication dates, and citations from third-party sources. A study found 85% of AI brand mentions originate from third-party sources.

### 5. Performance and Core Web Vitals
Slow pages get deprioritized by AI engines and Google AI Overviews. Target LCP under 2.5 seconds, CLS under 0.1, and TBT under 200ms.

## How AEO Differs Across Platforms

Each AI engine has slightly different behavior when selecting sources:

**ChatGPT Search** - heavily influenced by Bing's web index. Strong Bing rankings = better ChatGPT visibility. Named authorship and E-E-A-T signals matter a lot.

**Perplexity** - crawls the web in near real-time. Freshness matters more here than ChatGPT. Rewards content with clear question-format headings and frequently updated pages.

**Google AI Overviews** - pulls mostly from Google's existing top-10 results. Traditional SEO still applies, but content needs to be structured for snippet extraction.

**Microsoft Copilot** - relies heavily on Bing's index. Particularly useful for B2B queries. LinkedIn presence matters for brand entity recognition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is AEO replacing SEO?**

No. AEO builds on SEO - both reward high-quality, well-structured, authoritative content. You need both. SEO drives the organic traffic that pays the bills today. AEO builds the brand authority that protects your visibility as AI search grows.

**How long does AEO take to show results?**

For Perplexity, structural fixes like robots.txt and schema can show results within 2-6 weeks. Broader AI visibility across ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews typically takes 2-4 months of consistent effort.

**Do I need a big website to benefit from AEO?**

No. AI engines prioritize relevance and answer quality over brand size or domain authority. A small site with well-structured, authoritative content on a specific niche topic can outperform much larger sites in AI citations.

**What is the single most important AEO fix?**

Check your robots.txt and make sure PerplexityBot and ChatGPT-User are not blocked. This is the most common reason sites are completely invisible to AI search - and it takes 5 minutes to fix.
